PROJECT 02 · LINKEDINVLSI · CMOS / FinFET

Design and Simulation-Based Performance Optimization of a 90 nm CMOS Broadband Low-Noise Amplifier (LNA) Using Cadence Virtuoso

Designed and simulated a 90 nm CMOS broadband Low-Noise Amplifier (LNA) for wireless communication receivers, focusing on amplifying weak RF signals while minimizing added noise. Work included transistor-level CMOS circuit design, GPDK 90 nm technology, schematic and testbench development, transient-response simulation, and power and delay analysis.
